# Chapter 27 — Flight Controls

## GMM 27-10 Actuator slow response on BIT
Check hydraulic supply pressure first. Replace servo valve or actuator if response remains slow. Perform rigging check and full
functional test after replacement.

## GMM 27-20 Hinge and bracket corrosion
Coastal bases: inspect control-surface hinges every 15 days. Replace bushings with hinge bushing kit if corrosion is found under the bush.

## GMM 27-30 High-G operations
Aircraft flown in high-G training profiles accumulate actuator and hinge wear faster; apply the profile wear factor when planning inspections.
