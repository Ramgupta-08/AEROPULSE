# Chapter 28 — Fuel

## GMM 28-10 Fuel seepage from tank access panel
Classify leak rate (stain, seep, heavy seep, running leak). Reseal panel with tank sealant kit; running leaks ground the aircraft.

## GMM 28-20 Boost pump low pressure
Check circuit breaker and relay, measure pump output pressure. Replace boost pump if output is low.

## GMM 28-30 Microbial contamination
At humid bases, sample tank sumps weekly. Dark sludge indicates microbial growth — treat with approved biocide and replace filters.
