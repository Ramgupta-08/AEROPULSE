# Chapter 72 — Engine

## GMM 72-10 EGT rising trend or exceedance
Symptoms: exhaust gas temperature margin shrinking over successive sorties, spread between thermocouples, exceedance on take-off.
Likely causes:
1. Hot-section deterioration (HPT blade coating loss, nozzle guide vane erosion).
2. Compressor fouling or erosion — lower efficiency, higher fuel flow for same thrust.
3. Thermocouple harness fault — check EGT spread.
Recommended checks:
- Review HUMS trend: EGT, fuel flow and spool speeds over the last 20 sorties.
- Borescope HPT stage 1 blades and NGVs.
- Perform compressor wash and re-run trim; if EGT margin does not recover, plan module change.
A rising EGT trend combined with rising HPC outlet static pressure and fuel-flow ratio indicates core degradation; plan engine removal before the predicted failure point.

## GMM 72-20 High engine vibration
Check fan balance and trim weights, inspect engine mounts, take oil sample for spectrometric analysis. A rising vibration trend
with metal in oil indicates bearing distress — remove engine.

## GMM 72-30 Oil pressure fluctuation
Check oil level and filter, replace pressure transmitter if indication only. Low pressure with chip detector debris: remove engine.

## GMM 72-40 Compressor stall
Inspect inlet for FOD, borescope compressor, check variable stator vane rigging. Repeated stall requires engine change.
