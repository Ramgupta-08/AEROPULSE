# Chapter 32 — Landing Gear

## GMM 32-10 Corrosion on main gear axle, torque links and wheel well
Typical at coastal bases due to salt-laden air. Inspect after every 10 sorties at coastal bases.
Recommended actions:
1. Fresh-water wash of gear and wheel well after flying days with sea-spray exposure.
2. Remove light corrosion mechanically, apply inhibitor and touch-up paint.
3. Pitting beyond 0.25 mm depth on axle or torque link requires component replacement.
4. Apply corrosion-preventive compound (anti-corrosion kit) to wheel well fittings.

## GMM 32-20 Shock strut leak or low extension
Clean strut, measure extension against servicing chart. Leak at gland seal requires seal replacement; if extension cannot be
restored, replace shock strut. Duration 6–10 man-hours.

## GMM 32-40 Brake wear
Replace brake unit when wear pin is flush. Hot brakes after landing above limit require a cooling period and inspection for warping.

## GMM 32-50 Tyre damage
Cuts deeper than tread base or exposing cords require wheel assembly change.
